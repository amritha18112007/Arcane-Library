CHAPTER ONE

Commander Gray Pierce had been watching snow fall over Washington when the first explosion tore through the lower levels of Sigma Force headquarters. The building shuddered beneath him, lights flickered, and glass rained from the upper floors. The attack had begun with a precision that immediately told Gray this was no ordinary assault.

1
CRUCIBLE

Alarms clawed through the marble corridors as Gray moved toward the danger. Security teams were already converging on the damaged laboratory, but the scene below made no sense. The research chamber had been breached without destroying the equipment around it. Someone had entered for one thing and one thing only, and whatever they had taken was important enough to risk an entire military installation.

2
CHAPTER ONE

Inside the ruined laboratory, Gray found evidence of a carefully planned extraction. A prototype artificial-intelligence core had vanished while the surrounding machinery remained almost untouched. Someone had known exactly where to go, what to disable, and how long they had before the alarms reached the room.

3
CRUCIBLE

Another clue appeared. A mysterious woman had been seen fleeing the facility, carrying herself with the confidence of someone who already knew how the investigation would unfold. Documents left behind pointed toward an older secret, connected to forbidden knowledge and a conspiracy that reached far beyond modern technology.

4
CHAPTER ONE

Gray traced the clues backward and discovered that the trail did not begin in Washington. It passed through hidden archives, religious conflicts, vanished scholars, and organizations that had guarded dangerous knowledge long before computers existed. The Knights Templar appeared in the records, followed by references to a mysterious object called the crucible.

5
CRUCIBLE

Every answer created another question. Why had ancient guardians protected a secret connected to artificial intelligence? Why had someone waited centuries to retrieve it now? And why did the attack suggest that the people behind it understood Sigma Force almost as well as Gray did? The investigation was only beginning, but the first pieces of the puzzle were already moving into place.

THE YOUNG ELITES

Adelina Amouteru had learned that power could change the way the world looked at you. Once she had been a frightened girl marked by a deadly illness. Now the scars that remained on her skin were only one part of what made people fear her. Something darker lived beneath them, something she had learned to command.

1
THE ROSE SOCIETY

But power did not erase the memories of everything she had lost. The people she loved, the people who had betrayed her, and the future she had once imagined all followed her like shadows. Adelina understood that the world would never willingly give her what she wanted. If she wanted a place in it, she would have to take one.

2
THE YOUNG ELITES

The Rose Society gathered around a dangerous idea: the Young Elites did not need to hide forever. They could build something of their own, away from rulers who hunted them and ordinary people who feared them. Adelina saw the possibility of freedom, but she also saw an opportunity for revenge.

3
THE ROSE SOCIETY

As alliances formed, every promise carried a hidden cost. Friends became rivals, loyalties shifted, and the line between protecting the innocent and punishing the cruel became increasingly difficult to see. Adelina's greatest challenge was no longer discovering what she could do. It was deciding what she was willing to become.

4
THE YOUNG ELITES

The deeper Adelina entered the world of the Rose Society, the more she realized that fear could be useful. People who had once dismissed her now watched every move she made. Yet control built on fear could never be completely stable, especially when the people standing beside you had ambitions of their own.

5
THE ROSE SOCIETY

The future of the Young Elites would depend on choices made when trust was almost impossible. Adelina had spent years believing that strength meant never allowing anyone to hurt her again. Now she had to face a more dangerous question: what happens when the person you most need to defeat is the person you are becoming?

SHADOW AND BONE

2
BOOK ONE

SHADOW AND BONE

BOOK ONE

5
SHADOW AND BONE

THE DREGS

Ketterdam rewarded people who recognized an opportunity before anyone else did. Kaz Brekker had made a life out of finding those opportunities, especially when they involved impossible odds and questionable methods. Then he was offered a job so dangerous that even he knew it could not be completed alone.

1
SIX OF CROWS

The target was a military stronghold that was supposed to be impossible to enter and even harder to escape. The prize was not simply money. A scientist held a secret that could change the balance of power, and someone was willing to pay a fortune to bring him out alive.

2
THE DREGS

Kaz assembled a crew whose skills did not fit neatly together. Inej could move through a building like a shadow. Jesper's confidence was matched by his skill with a weapon. Nina understood the power of the Grisha, while Matthias carried knowledge of the enemy they were about to face. Wylan brought talents of his own, even if he did not always believe in them.

3
SIX OF CROWS

They were not friends, and trust was not part of the agreement. Each member had a reason for accepting the job, and each reason was personal. The closer they came to the fortress, the more important it became to understand not only what the others could do, but what they were willing to risk.

4
THE DREGS

The plan depended on timing, deception, and a great deal of nerve. One mistake could lock the entire crew inside a fortress designed to keep people from escaping. Kaz knew that every plan eventually met something unexpected. The real skill was making the unexpected part of the plan.

5
SIX OF CROWS

As the crew moved deeper into enemy territory, old grudges and hidden loyalties began surfacing. The heist was becoming more than a question of money. It was becoming a test of whether six people who had survived by trusting nobody could work together long enough to survive.

DAY ONE

4
THE ACADEMY

5
THE POPPY WAR

2
THE FINAL EMPIRE

3
MISTBORN

4
THE FINAL EMPIRE

5
MISTBORN

As Vin learned more about the Empire's history, she began to wonder whether its greatest secret had been hidden from the skaa all along.
